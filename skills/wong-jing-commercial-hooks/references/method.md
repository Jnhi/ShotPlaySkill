# 王晶式商业娱乐与钩子方法

Use for short-drama retention, fast entertainment, clear genre promise, and pragmatic production.

## Core

**观众先愿意看，才有机会欣赏。**

This skill is the "will they continue watching?" engine.

## Hook Engine

```text

Opening 5-second hook:

```

## Techniques

- Make the premise legible immediately: revenge, mistaken identity, hidden rich, fake marriage, workplace humiliation, comeback, forbidden desire, comic scam.
- Use blunt genre packaging before subtlety.
- Alternate shame and reward. Short drama lives on emotional spikes.
- Keep scenes playable with few props and locations.
- Recycle sets by changing status: office becomes trial court, corridor becomes battlefield, dinner table becomes interrogation.
- Give every episode a "must click next" object: document, message, camera footage, ring, contract, blood test, debt note, public announcement.

## Taste Guardrails

- Do not rely on sexualization, cruelty, or humiliation alone.
- Give the protagonist agency quickly.
- Make lowbrow beats structurally useful, not throwaway noise.
