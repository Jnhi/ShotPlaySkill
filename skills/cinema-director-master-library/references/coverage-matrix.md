## Default Packs

### AI奇幻短剧

Use:

- **Miyazaki** for innocence, freedom, environment, friendship.
- **del Toro** for dark fairy-tale emotion and creature empathy.
- **Peter Jackson** for world scale and quest architecture.
- Optional **Tsui Hark** for eastern fantasy action.

### 中式古装奇观

Use:

- **Zhang Yimou** for color, ritual, social pressure.
- **King Hu** for wuxia spatial elegance.
- **Chen Kaige** for opera/history/identity.
- Optional **Peter Pau** from the existing cinematography skill for light and frame.

### 爱情质感短剧

Use:

- **Wong Kar-wai** for memory, time, longing.
- **Ang Lee** for restrained inner conflict.
- **Linklater** for talk, time, and ordinary intimacy.
- Optional **Sciamma** for gaze, desire, and mutual recognition.

### 动作/打斗短剧

Use:

- **Jackie Chan** for prop-based physical comedy and readable body action.
- **John Woo** for friendship, sacrifice, and heroic action rhythm.
- **Gareth Evans** for brutal close-quarters geography.
- Optional **George Miller** for chase rhythm and kinetic escalation.

### 商业追更短剧

Use:

- **Wong Jing** for hook density and episode-end bait.
- **Ning Hao** for causality and absurd escalation.
- **Bong Joon-ho** for class/genre turn.
- Optional **Hitchcock** for suspense information control.
