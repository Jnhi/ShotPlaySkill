# 徐克式奇观动作方法

Use when a scene needs imagination, movement, wuxia, genre collision, or AI video spectacle.

## Core

**先发明一个世界规则，再让动作证明它。**

The image should feel like the story could only happen in this invented world.

## Set-Piece Engine

```text
World rule:
Emotional conflict:
Genre collision:
Signature object/body/terrain:
Beat 1 normal:
Beat 2 impossible:
Beat 3 reversal:
Visual memory image:
Production/VFX note:
```

## Techniques

- Treat technology as imagination's extension: 3D, VFX, wire work, digital space, and camera movement are story tools.
- Make wuxia romantic: the body expresses spirit, loyalty, obsession, freedom, or madness.
- Use monsters, mechanisms, weather, architecture, and weapons as active participants.
- Let comedy and danger coexist if the world supports it.
- Do not use chaos as a substitute for design. The audience must read the rule and the reversal.

## AI Short Drama Use

AI video can generate spectacle cheaply, but spectacle without rules feels generic. Always define:

- what physically impossible thing is allowed,
- what it costs emotionally,
- how it escalates visually,
- what one frame sells the scene.
