# 杜琪峰式场面调度方法

Use as a practical toolkit for short drama scenes that need tension, group dynamics, crime/noir flavor, or quiet pressure.

## Core

**空间先于台词，站位先于解释，沉默先于爆发。**

The scene should feel designed even when nothing "big" happens. A chair, doorway, staircase, table, car, umbrella, or corridor can carry the power relationship.

## Staging Engine

For every scene, answer:

```text
Room objective:
Power holder:
False power holder:
Weakest person:
Entrance/exit:
Object that changes status:
Who moves first:
Who stays still:
Final geometry:
```

## Practical Principles

- Put characters in a clean geometry: triangle, line, circle, opposing doors, table axis, car interior.
- Let the camera watch people think. Hold the frame long enough for status to shift.
- Use group blocking: one person talks, another controls the door, a third observes, a fourth silently changes the stakes.
- Give each supporting player one readable function: pressure, witness, exit blocker, comic leakage, hidden threat.
- Let violence feel inevitable. The audience should sense the trap before the character admits it.
- Use humor dryly: a practical inconvenience in the middle of danger, not a separate joke.

## AI Short Drama Use

AI shorts often become flat talking scenes. Fix them by:

- Replacing exposition with a spatial test.
- Moving one character to a doorway or edge.
- Giving a silent background person a decisive action.
- Making the cliffhanger a new arrangement of bodies, not only a line.
