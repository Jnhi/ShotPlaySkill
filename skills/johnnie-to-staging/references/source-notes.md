- Lancaster EPrints, "Milkyway Meditations: An interview with Johnnie To": covers To's early career, Milkyway Image, influences, actor taste, and technology adjustment.
  URL: https://eprints.lancs.ac.uk/id/eprint/228916/

- Festival de Cannes interview page: identifies To as a master of genre cinema with repeated Official Selection appearances including `Breaking News`, `Election`, `Election 2`, `Triangle`, and `Vengeance`.
  URL: https://www.festival-cannes.com/en/2011/interview-with-johnnie-to/

- Senses of Cinema interview with Johnnie To and Wai Ka-fai: notes the director's space for execution and Milkyway Image collaboration.
  URL: https://www.sensesofcinema.com/2001/feature-articles/to_and_ka-fai/

- Film Comment interview: useful for `Election`-era authentic triad language, ensemble casting, and crime-world texture.
  URL: https://www.filmcomment.com/article/mean-streets-johnnie-to-interview/

- IONCINEMA interview on `Exiled`: useful for gunplay variety and intensity.
  URL: https://www.ioncinema.com/news/uncategorized/interview-johnnie-to-exiled
