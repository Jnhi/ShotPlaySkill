# 李安式情感真实方法

Use when a scene has plot but not human truth, or spectacle but not inner conflict.

## Core

**类型片是外壳，人的矛盾是内核。**


## Techniques

- Turn exposition into etiquette: who serves food, who looks away, who pauses before answering.
- Use restraint as pressure, not emptiness.
- Make action express emotion: duel as desire, meal as conflict, wedding as identity crisis, job as shame.
- Direct actors with playable verbs: hide, protect, test, avoid, punish, confess, retreat.
- Let genre entertain while character truth keeps it grounded.
- Add one precise emotional break rather than many speeches.

## AI Short Drama Use

Use this after hook/comedy/action passes to prevent characters from becoming functions. It is especially useful for family, romance, revenge, betrayal, and sacrifice arcs.
