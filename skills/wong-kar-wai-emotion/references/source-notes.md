# Source Notes

- KPBS interview with Wong Kar-wai: notes his use of story outlines rather than complete scripts, daily discovery on set, and suggestive rather than explanatory scenes.
  URL: https://www.kpbs.org/news/arts-culture/2003/03/30/interview-wong-kar-wai

- The Independent interview: Wong explains mood and tempo can be communicated through music better than a script.
  URL: https://www.the-independent.com/arts-entertainment/films/features/the-fine-art-of-stripping-5368738.html

- Filmmaker Magazine interview on `Chungking Express`: useful for low-permit, fast, location-driven production and difficulty explaining a mood-based Hong Kong story.
  URL: https://filmmakermagazine.com/107448-wong-kar-wai-chungking-express/

- Moving Image Source transcript: discusses existing light, shutter/time effects, and the practical side of mood filmmaking.
  URL: https://www.movingimagesource.us/files/dialogues/3/71112_programs_transcript_html_303.htm

- Interview Magazine on `The Grandmaster`: useful for martial arts as impressionistic dance and Wong's highly stylized visual tradition.
  URL: https://www.interviewmagazine.com/film/wong-kar-wai-the-grandmaster

Treat "Wong Kar-wai-inspired" as a method for mood, memory, and time, not a request to copy famous lines.
