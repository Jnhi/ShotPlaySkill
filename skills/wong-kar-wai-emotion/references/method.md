# 王家卫式情绪与时间方法

Use when a short drama needs atmosphere, intimacy, longing, urban melancholy, or lyrical fragmentation.

## Core

**故事可以很少，情绪必须很准。**

The engine is not "what happens next" but "what did they fail to say, and what does time do to that failure?"

## Mood Engine

```text
Unspoken desire:

Time marker:
Music/ambient rhythm:

Final residue:
```

## Techniques

- Replace plot explanation with repeated routines: buying food, waiting, walking a corridor, looking at a clock, cleaning a table.
- Use objects as emotional storage: ticket, key, food, cigarette, raincoat, expired item, elevator, empty chair.
- Make voiceover say one thing while the image reveals another.
- Let two people share a space but not a direct confession.
- Use time stamps, dates, weather, and repeated locations to make memory physical.
- Let editing find the story: fragments can orbit the wound before revealing it.

## AI Short Drama Use

Use this skill after a commercial hook pass, not before, if the piece must still move fast. It can make a short feel expensive emotionally without adding sets.
